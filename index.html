Welcome!

Murray Lei is an Associate Professor of Management Analytics, a Distinguished Research Fellow, and the Academic Director of the Master of Management in Artificial Intelligence (MMAI) program at the Smith School of Business, Queen’s University. His work focuses on helping organizations use data, analytics, and AI to make better business decisions in complex and uncertain environments.

Murray’s research develops data-driven approaches that enable organizations to adapt their decisions as new information becomes available. He also examines how organizations can pursue business objectives while addressing privacy, fairness, and sustainability. His work has been published in leading peer-reviewed journals, supported by multiple funding agencies, and recognized through several awards, including the New Researcher Achievement Award.

Murray develops new executive education programs and brings extensive teaching experience across executive education and professional graduate programs. His expertise spans business analytics, artificial intelligence, operations and supply chain management, and revenue management. He created and taught Generative AI for Business, Smith’s first course dedicated to generative AI, and led the MMAI program’s first curriculum redesign since its launch in 2018. He has been named Professor of the Year by students in the Master of Management Analytics program.

Murray translates this expertise into practice through consulting with both large and small organizations on AI adoption, from identifying opportunities aligned with business needs to leading the deployment of AI solutions. He has also collaborated with leading companies, including Alibaba, Instacart, Flipkart, Staples, and Oracle Retail Labs, contributing to the development of real-time, large-scale analytics solutions.
